The Property

A villa built for slow mornings
and golden evenings

Set on a quiet ridge above Malta's northern coastline, Villa Aurora pairs traditional limestone architecture with a fully modern interior. Sea views stretch from the infinity pool across to Comino and Gozo, and the honey-coloured stone walls glow at sunset — the kind of light Malta is known for.

Just minutes from the fishing village of Mellieħa and the white sands of Golden Bay, the villa sits close enough to explore the island's harbours and historic towns, yet private enough to feel like your own corner of the Mediterranean.

Where You'll Be

The Location

Villa Aurora sits above Malta's rugged northwest coast, between the limestone cliffs of Mellieħa and the turquoise waters of Golden Bay. The walled city of Mdina, Malta's former capital, is a short drive inland, while the Baroque harbours of Valletta and the ferry to Gozo and Comino's Blue Lagoon are both within easy reach for a day trip.
